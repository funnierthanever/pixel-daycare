Pixel Daycare

Tap a Pokémon on the field to say hi. Tap 🔨 to build your own daycare.

Visit friends

Share your house: anyone with the link can visit it in a browser, or paste it into the app. Visitors see a snapshot of your house as it is right now, with your Pokémon living their lives.

You're visiting. Nothing here is saved, and your own house is untouched.

Bring in your Pokémon

Pick a save file (from Files / iCloud, or drop it on the page). Your party and PC boxes show up, and you choose who moves in (up to 100). Works with Gen 1–5 battery saves: .sav, .srm, .dsv. The file is read on this device and never uploaded.

Residents

Showing the area you're in. Pokémon wander between areas on their own; use ▦ (the PC) to see where everyone is.
